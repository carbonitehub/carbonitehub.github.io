How to Activate Carbonite

Activating Carbonite is simple. Install the latest version of the software, sign in with your registered Carbonite account, and enter your activation or license key when prompted. Once verified, your subscription will be activated, allowing you to begin backing up your files securely. If your activation key is not accepted, verify that it matches your subscription and ensure you are connected to the internet during activation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I activate Carbonite?

Install Carbonite, sign in with your account, enter your activation key, and follow the on-screen instructions to complete activation.

Where can I find my activation key?

Your activation key is typically included in your purchase confirmation email or available in your Carbonite account.

Why is my activation key not working?

Double-check that the key is entered correctly, verify your subscription is active, and ensure you are using the correct account.

Can I activate Carbonite on a new computer?

Yes. Sign in to your Carbonite account, install the software on the new device, and activate it using your existing subscription if your plan allows.
